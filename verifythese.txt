1a. Verbs and question words  (data/action_lexicon.json -> "verbs")
    Patterns are regular expressions: the stem plus endings; up to 10 letters of
    prefixes before the stem are allowed (siz(a|e|...) matches siza, ungisize).
    [ ] open     patterns: vul(?:a|e|ele|ela|ile|eni)
              English words: open
    [ ] show     patterns: bonis(?:a|e|ele|ile), khombis(?:a|e|ele|ile)
              English words: show
    [ ] find     patterns: thol(?:a|e|ile|eni), fik(?:a|e|ile), kanjani
              English words: find, get to, go to, how do i get
    [ ] contact  patterns: shayel(?:a|e|ile), thint(?:a|e|ile)
              English words: call, contact
    [ ] teach    patterns: fundis(?:a|e|ile)(?:yo)?
              English words: teach, teaches, lecture
    [ ] tell     patterns: tshel(?:a|e|ele|ile|eni), chaz(?:a|e|ele|ile), buz(?:a|e|ile)
              English words: tell, explain, ask
    [ ] where    patterns: kuphi, [ilsz]iphi
              English words: where
    [ ] when     patterns: nini
              English words: when, what time
    [ ] who      patterns: ngubani, obani
              English words: who
    [ ] what     patterns: sini, yini, yimuphi, yiziphi
              English words: what, which
    [ ] help     patterns: siz(?:a|e|ele|ela|ile|eni|o)
              English words: help, assist
    [x] do       patterns: enz(?:a|e|ele|ela|ile|anga|eni)
              English words: do   (from your verified pairs)
